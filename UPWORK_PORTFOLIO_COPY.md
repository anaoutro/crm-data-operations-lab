# Paste-ready Upwork portfolio entries

## 1. Public B2B Lead Research & Data Entry QA

**Title:** B2B Account Research | Verified Public Sources, Data Entry QA & CRM-Ready Export

**Role:** Research, data cleaning, Python automation and CRM data architecture

**Description:** I researched seven real service businesses using their official websites and built a reproducible company-level prospecting workflow. Each record retains its source URL, observed booking route, market and evidence note. A Python pipeline normalizes names, catches duplicates and missing evidence, produces a review queue and prepares a CRM account import draft. The sample run has seven unique researched accounts and no quality exceptions. Buyer identities, buying intent and sales results were not assumed.

**Skills:** Lead Generation, B2B Marketing, Data Entry, Data Cleaning, Python, CRM Data Entry

**Label:** Independent portfolio research, not a client campaign.

**Visual cover:** [Editable Figma command center](https://www.figma.com/design/tvh5ZHY8mLNGKdh3v6eKgM/?node-id=19-2) with the seven real account names and the research-only metrics. The cover is a designed presentation of the underlying workbook, not a screenshot of a deployed client application.

## 2. Automated Excel Account Intelligence

**Title:** Automated Excel Lead Intelligence | Live QA, Research Scoring & Segment Plan

**Role:** Spreadsheet design, formulas and data validation

**Description:** I built an editable Excel workbook using public data from seven businesses. It calculates unique account counts, duplicate flags, research-priority scores and the next review action as inputs change. A linked B2B plan counts companies by segment and gives the researcher a discovery question for each segment. The score prioritizes research; it does not claim purchase intent. A duplicate test changed the unique count from seven to six and restored correctly after the source row was reset.

**Skills:** Microsoft Excel, Excel Automation, Data Entry, Data Validation, Lead Research, B2B Marketing

**Label:** Independent portfolio demonstration with real public company data.

**Workbook:** `Public_B2B_Lead_Intelligence_2026.xlsx`.
